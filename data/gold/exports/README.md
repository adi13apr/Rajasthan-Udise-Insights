# District-level exports

District x year aggregates from the gold marts (`sql/11_marts_district.sql`), for
Tableau and Excel. Generated by `python run_pipeline.py`; do not edit by hand.

- `mart_district_year.csv`: one row per district and year.
- `mart_district_year_management.csv`: one row per district, year and management group.

Schools with no students in classes 1-12 are excluded. Column definitions:
`docs/data_dictionary.md`.

Source: UDISE+, Ministry of Education, Government of India. School-level data is not published.
